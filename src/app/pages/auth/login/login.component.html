Login / Logout

AuthController.login(request) is public: the service looks for the email (GET /people?email=...), compares the password, and puts the user in AuthService.currentUser. AuthController.logout() (logged in only) empties it. Every page, controller and the guard just read currentUser().

The navbar toggle is a demo helper: after a refresh it logs Bob back in if it is on. Your real login here lives in memory only (a refresh forgets it).
